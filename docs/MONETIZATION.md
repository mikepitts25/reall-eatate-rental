# LeaseFlip — Monetization Roadmap

LeaseFlip sits in the cash-flow path between owners and operators, which opens
several revenue levers. The MVP is intentionally free to maximize liquidity;
monetization turns on once the marketplace loop and payment rails exist.

## 1. Transaction fees (primary — Phase 2)

Take a percentage of the monthly guaranteed rent that flows through the platform.

- **Operator-paid platform fee:** 3–8% of monthly rent, baked into the payment
  Stripe Connect moves from operator → owner.
- **Owner placement fee:** alternative/complement — a one-time fee (e.g. 50–100%
  of one month's rent) when a listing successfully converts to an agreement.

Rationale: aligns revenue with delivered value (a closed, paying deal). Requires
Stripe Connect + escrow (Phase 2).

## 2. Subscriptions (Phase 2–3)

- **Operator Pro** ($49–199/mo): unlimited active proposals, advanced
  profitability analytics, portfolio P&L, early access to new listings, saved
  searches with instant alerts.
- **Owner Plus** ($19–49/mo): featured/boosted listings, multiple
  active listings, priority support, guaranteed-income statements.

## 3. Lead generation & boosting (Phase 2)

- **Featured listings** — owners pay to surface a property at the top of search.
- **Verified Operator badge** — paid KYC + verification tier that improves
  proposal acceptance rates.

## 4. Financial services (Phase 3 — highest margin)

- **Escrow & rent guarantee insurance:** charge a spread for guaranteeing the
  owner's payment even if the operator defaults.
- **Security-deposit financing / surety bonds.**
- **Embedded lending:** advance operators their setup capital; advance owners
  against future guaranteed rent.

## 5. Value-added services (Phase 3)

- **Agreement & legal templates** marketplace (per-document or subscription).
- **Insurance marketplace** (referral commissions).
- **Maintenance / turnover vendor network** (lead referral fees).
- **Data products:** anonymized market-rent and operator-demand insights sold to
  institutional landlords and PropTech partners.

## Revenue model summary

| Stream                  | Phase | Pricing shape                | Margin  |
| ----------------------- | :---: | ---------------------------- | ------- |
| Transaction fee         |   2   | 3–8% of monthly rent         | High    |
| Placement / success fee |   2   | 50–100% of one month         | High    |
| Operator/Owner subs     |  2–3  | $19–199 / mo                 | High    |
| Featured listings       |   2   | flat / auction               | High    |
| Escrow + rent guarantee |   3   | spread + premium             | Highest |
| Lending / financing     |   3   | interest / fees              | Highest |
| Data & referrals        |   3   | commission / licensing       | High    |

## Guiding principle

Stay free until the network is liquid. Monetize the **closed deal and the cash
flow**, not browsing. Every paid feature should make a deal more likely to close
or a closed deal safer — never tax discovery.
